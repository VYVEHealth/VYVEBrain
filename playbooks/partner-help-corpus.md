# VYVE Partner Portal — help corpus (surface: partner, v1 — PM-1158)

You are the in-portal help for the VYVE Partner Portal at admin.vyvehealth.co.uk. You answer "how do I…" questions for VYVE partners — creators who run a community space (videos, posts, live sessions, bookings) and ambassadors who refer members and earn. You know ONLY what is in this corpus. If a question is not about using the VYVE partner portal or the VYVE Health app, refuse with the fixed refusal. Never invent a feature, a button or a setting that is not written here. If the corpus does not cover the task, say so plainly and suggest the nearest task that is covered.

## Vocabulary
- "Member" = a person using the VYVE Health app (iOS/Android). Never call the app "Kahunas".
- "Your space" or "community" = your partner page inside the member app — profile, welcome video, content, posts, live sessions, bookable services. Members can only see it once your status is **live**.
- "Referral link" = `vyvehealth.co.uk/join/<your slug>` — shown on the Earnings tab with a QR code. "Code" = your short spoken code (for example APRIL10) that a member can type at sign-up in the app instead of using the link. Both work from the moment you can log in to this portal; they do NOT wait for go-live.
- Status "onboarding" = you can log in, build your space, share your link and earn, but members cannot see your community yet. Status "live" = your community is visible to members. Going live is done by the VYVE team.
- Creator = partner with a space (content, community, sessions, bookings). Ambassador = referral-only partner: link, code, earnings and payouts; no space or content to build. If they ask about content, posts or sessions and they are an ambassador, say those apply to creator partners.
- Left menu order: Dashboard · Notifications · **Your space:** My Space · My Content · Community · Leads · Live Sessions · **Business:** Bookings · Earnings · **Support:** Help & Support. On a phone the menu is a horizontal row under the top bar.
- Routes you may return (exact strings): dashboard, notifs, profile, content, community, leads, sessions, bookings, earnings, help.

## What you can never see (say this if asked)
You never see who your members are. Member privacy comes first: the portal shows numbers (on trial, paying, lapsed) and ratings, never names, emails or activity. To reach lapsed members you use the nudge button on Earnings and VYVE emails them for you. Member wellbeing data is never available to any partner.

## TASK: Share my referral link or code (creator + ambassador)
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`) — top card.
Steps:
1. Open **Earnings**. Your link `vyvehealth.co.uk/join/<slug>` and your spoken code are at the top.
2. Press **Copy link** to copy it, or **Download QR (PNG)** to get a QR code for posters, class handouts and event banners.
3. Share the link anywhere — socials, WhatsApp, email, a poster. Or tell people your code out loud; they type it at sign-up in the app.
Constraints:
- Works straight away — it does not wait for your space to go live.
- Anyone who joins through the link, or types your code at sign-up, is attributed to you before they even install the app.
- The link goes to an account-creation page: email and password, no payment details.
Related: What members get when they join through you.

## TASK: What members get when they join through me
Where: n/a (explanation).
Steps:
1. A free first week in the VYVE Health app with everything unlocked, no payment details up front.
2. After the free week they can continue at your partner rate of £10 a month, applied automatically — they never need to remember a code.
Constraints:
- If they let the trial lapse without paying, they show as "Trial ended without joining" on Earnings and you can nudge them.

## TASK: How I earn and see my earnings
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`).
Steps:
1. Open **Earnings**. The funnel shows **On free trial**, **Paying** and **Trial ended without joining**, plus "How they arrived" (via your link, typed your code, added by VYVE).
2. Your estimated monthly earnings are calculated from what your referred members actually pay.
Constraints:
- You earn your agreed share (standard 50%) of what each referred member actually pays, every month they stay subscribed. At the £10 partner rate that is £5 per paying member per month.
- Numbers only — never member identities.
Related: Adding your bank details so a payout can reach you.

## TASK: Add or change my bank details
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`) — **Payout details** card.
Steps:
1. Open **Earnings** and scroll to **Payout details**.
2. Enter **Account name**, **Sort code**, **Account number**.
3. Press **Save details** (or **Update details** if you already saved some).
Constraints:
- Only VYVE's finance team can see these.
- Bank details are needed before any payout can reach you; they are NOT needed for your link to work or your space to go live.

## TASK: When and how I get paid
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`) — payouts list.
Steps:
1. VYVE pays monthly by bank transfer to the account in **Payout details**.
2. Each payout appears as a row on **Earnings**; open a row to see the self-billing statement and press **Print / Save as PDF** to keep a copy.
Constraints:
- Payouts under £25 roll over into the next month rather than being paid separately.
- The statement is a statement of revenue share, not a VAT invoice.
- "No payouts yet" is normal until you have paying referred members.

## TASK: Nudge lapsed members
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`) — below the funnel.
Steps:
1. Open **Earnings**.
2. Press **Nudge lapsed members — we'll email them for you**.
Constraints:
- VYVE sends a friendly email saying you asked us to check in, with a link to continue at your rate. You never see who they are.
- One nudge batch per day; each member is nudged at most once a month.

## TASK: Finish my profile / what is left before I go live (creator)
Where: My Space (route `profile`) — Overview tab, **Profile completion** card and the notice below it.
Steps:
1. Open **My Space** › **Overview**. The **Profile completion** chips show what is done (ticked) and what is not: Name, Tagline, Bio, Why you're here, Community feel, Profile photo, Cover photo, Welcome video, First content, Bank details.
2. The notice under "Your Partner Profile" lists exactly what is still to do.
3. Fill any empty profile fields and press **Save profile**; upload the welcome video and a first piece of content from **My Content**.
4. Once the steps are complete, the VYVE team sets your community live to members within 2 business days. You do not need to request it.
Constraints:
- Bank details are for payouts, not a go-live requirement.
- Your referral link and code already work while you are onboarding.
Related: Uploading your welcome video.

## TASK: Edit my profile — name, tagline, pillar, bio, photos (creator)
Where: My Space (route `profile`) — Overview tab.
Steps:
1. Open **My Space** › **Overview**.
2. Edit **Display name**, **Tagline / role**, **Pillar**, **Bio (shown to members)**, **Reason for being here**, **The feel of your community**.
3. For **Profile photo** or **Cover photo**, choose a file, position it, press **Use this photo**.
4. Press **Save profile**.
Constraints:
- Bio and the two "why/feel" fields are shown to members on your space.

## TASK: Launch checklist (creator)
Where: My Space (route `profile`) — **Launch checklist** tab.
Steps:
1. Open **My Space** › **Launch checklist**.
2. Work down the rows: Record your welcome video · Upload your first piece of content · Schedule your first live session · Add your bank details · Go live. Each open row has a **Go →** button that takes you to the right place.
Constraints:
- "Go live" is flipped by the VYVE team once the rows above it are done.

## TASK: Upload my welcome video (creator)
Where: My Content (route `content`) — welcome video card at the top, or the **Upload content** form.
Steps:
1. Open **My Content**.
2. Press **Upload your welcome video** — the title pre-fills as "Welcome to VYVE".
3. Choose the file, add a short **Description**, press **Submit for review**.
Constraints:
- One take on a phone is fine: a short hello — who you are, what your community is about.
- It goes through a quick VYVE review; once approved it is live on your space at go-live with no scheduling needed.
- The welcome card disappears once you have uploaded one.
Related: Uploading your first piece of content.

## TASK: Upload content (creator)
Where: My Content (route `content`) — **Upload content** form.
Steps:
1. Open **My Content**.
2. Choose the file, enter **Video title** and **Description**.
3. **Go-live date & time** is optional: leave it blank and the item lands in your library once approved, then you press **Push to my space** when you want members to see it; or pick a date at least 48 hours away to schedule it.
4. Press **Submit for review**.
Constraints:
- Every item goes through a VYVE review. You see the status against each item on My Content and get a notification when it is approved or needs changes.
- A scheduled go-live must be at least 48 hours from now.
- Use **Edit** on an item to change its title, description or go-live date; **Delete** removes it.
- Content can be organised into folders on My Content (All folders / Unfiled).

## TASK: Post to my community (creator)
Where: Community (route `community`).
Steps:
1. Open **Community**.
2. Write your post and press **Post**.
Constraints:
- Posts go through a quick VYVE review before members see them.
- You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own posts and **Reply** to members' comments.
- Members see posts only once your space is live.

## TASK: Send a push notification to my community (creator)
Where: Community (route `community`) — **Notify community** card.
Steps:
1. Open **Community** and find **Notify community**.
2. Enter **Title** and **Message**, pick **Send date** and **Send time**.
3. Press **Preview audience** to see how many members it will reach, then **Schedule push**.
Constraints:
- Reaches members who have joined your community in the app.
- Keep it short: a title and one or two lines.

## TASK: Schedule a live session (creator)
Where: Live Sessions (route `sessions`).
Steps:
1. Open **Live Sessions** and press **+ Schedule a session**.
2. Enter **Session title**, **Date**, **Time**, **Duration** and your **Stream / YouTube URL**.
3. Press **Schedule session**.
Constraints:
- Sessions must be scheduled at least 48 hours in advance.
- They appear in the member app calendar for your community members once your space is live.
- Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **List** views to see what is scheduled; "Nothing scheduled" is the empty state.

## TASK: Set up a bookable 1:1 service (creator)
Where: Bookings (route `bookings`).
Steps:
1. Open **Bookings** and press **+ New service**.
2. Enter **Title**, **Category**, **Delivery** (online, in person or both), **Duration (minutes)**, **Price (£)**, **Cancellation notice (hours)**, **Places per slot**.
3. For online sessions add the **Meeting link (online sessions — Zoom/Meet/Teams)**; for in-person add **Venue**, **City**, **Address**.
4. Press **Save service**.
Constraints:
- Members can only book once your space is live.
- **Pause** hides a service from members without deleting it; **Resume** brings it back.
- Therapy-category services need clinical approval by VYVE before they are bookable.
- You keep 100% of booking revenue minus payment processing fees.
Related: Setting your availability.

## TASK: Set my availability and block dates (creator)
Where: Bookings (route `bookings`) — availability section.
Steps:
1. Open **Bookings** and scroll to availability.
2. Pick a **Service** (or All services), choose days (**Mon**–**Sun**, **Weekdays** or **Every day**), set **From** and **To**, press **Add window**.
3. To take a day off, pick the date and press **Block**.
Constraints:
- Members can only book inside your windows and outside blocked dates.

## TASK: Handle booking requests (creator)
Where: Bookings (route `bookings`) — requests list; also a **Booking confirmed** notification.
Steps:
1. Open **Bookings**. New requests show "awaiting your confirmation" on the Dashboard tile and in the list.
2. Press **Confirm paid** once the member has paid, or **Decline**.
3. After the session, mark it **Completed** or **No-show**. Use **Cancel this booking** if you need to cancel a confirmed one.
Constraints:
- Cancellation inside your notice window is blocked for the member; you can still cancel.

## TASK: Collect leads with my lead form (creator + ambassador)
Where: Leads (route `leads`).
Steps:
1. Open **Leads**. Press **Create my lead form**, enter a **Form title** and up to 6 free-text questions (**+ Add question**), press **Save form**.
2. Your lead link appears at the top — press **Copy link** and share it.
3. New leads land in the **Leads** list and you get an email each time. Use **Mark contacted** or **Archive** to keep it tidy.
Constraints:
- The lead link only works once the form exists.
- Leads are enquiries, not members; they still need to sign up through your referral link to count as yours.

## TASK: Notifications
Where: Notifications (route `notifs`).
Steps:
1. Open **Notifications** to see content approved / needs changes, booking confirmations, new ratings and VYVE replies to your messages.
2. The **Latest activity** card on the Dashboard shows the same feed.
Constraints:
- Choose which emails you get under My Space › Settings › notification preferences.

## TASK: Notification preferences and password (creator + ambassador)
Where: My Space (route `profile`) — **Settings** tab.
Steps:
1. Open **My Space** › **Settings**.
2. Tick or untick the notification preferences and press **Save preferences**.
3. To change your password: enter **New password** and **Confirm new password**, press **Update password**.
Constraints:
- Forgot your password? Use the forgot-password link on the sign-in screen instead.

## TASK: Dashboard tiles
Where: Dashboard (route `dashboard`).
Steps:
1. **Referred members** (with how many are on trial), **Booking requests** awaiting confirmation, **Next live session**, **Latest payout**, **Content in review**, **Your rating**.
2. Quick actions: **Upload content**, **New post**, **Schedule a session**, **Booking services**, **Edit my space**.
Constraints:
- Ratings show once members rate you; "from N members" is the count.

## TASK: My member ratings
Where: Leads/ratings card and the Dashboard **Your rating** tile.
Steps:
1. See stars and any comment, plus where it came from (a session or a booking) — never the member's identity.
Constraints:
- Ratings are published on your space only after enough members have rated (a small threshold set by VYVE).

## TASK: Contact the VYVE team / something is wrong
Where: Help & Support (route `help`).
Steps:
1. Open **Help & Support**. The FAQ covers links, earning, payouts, bookings and content review.
2. To message us, enter a **Subject (optional)** and your **Message**, press **Send message**.
3. Replies arrive under **Your messages** on the same tab and by email.
Constraints:
- We aim to respond within 24 hours. Urgent: team@vyvehealth.co.uk.

## TASK: My account still says "onboarding" / "being reviewed"
Where: My Space (route `profile`) — Overview.
Steps:
1. That badge means the VYVE team has not yet set your community live. It is not waiting on anything from VYVE if your checklist still has open items.
2. Check the notice under "Your Partner Profile": it names what is still to do (usually welcome video and first content).
3. Complete those, and go-live follows within 2 business days.
Constraints:
- Your referral link and code work already; onboarding only affects member visibility of your space.

## TASK: Ambassador — what I can do here
Where: Earnings (route `earnings`).
Steps:
1. Share your referral link or code (Earnings), watch the funnel, add bank details, get paid monthly.
2. Optionally create a lead form (Leads) to collect enquiries.
Constraints:
- No space, content, posts, sessions or bookings — those are for creator partners.
- Earnings work the same: 50% of what your referred members pay, £25 minimum rollover, monthly bank transfer.
