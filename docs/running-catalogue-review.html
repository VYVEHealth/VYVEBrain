VYVE Running Plans — review draft

Four plan templates and seventeen session templates, drafted 13 September 2026 (PM-1240, Wave 1). Nothing here is live to members yet — the member-facing running surface is Wave 3.

Calum — what I need from you. This is a first draft written to get something concrete in front of you rather than a blank page. Correct it freely; nothing is precious.
  • Is the weekly shape right for each plan — the mix of long run, quality and easy running?
  • Is the progression sensible? The × figure on each cell is a volume multiplier applied to that session's base distance, so the week totals in the right-hand column are what a member actually runs.
  • Are the session structures right — rep distances, rest lengths, warm-up and cool-down?
  • Are the names and coach notes yours? Your name goes on the recommendation, so nothing carries it until you say so.
  • For Start Running specifically: is the eight-week run/walk ramp to thirty continuous minutes about right, or too fast for a true beginner?
How the paces work. Sessions never store a pace figure — they reference one of five bands, and the band is resolved per member from their race time. A member who runs 25:00 for 5k sees easy 6:36–7:40/km, long 6:48–7:56/km, threshold 5:24–5:44/km, interval 4:45–5:01/km, rep 4:29–4:40/km. A faster member sees faster numbers from exactly the same session. Limit means "no faster than this"; target means "run at this".

What scales and what doesn't. Repeated efforts hold their named distance at every volume setting — 800s are always 800m, a stride is always 100m, a broken mile is always a mile. Warm-ups, cool-downs, easy runs and long runs absorb the week multiplier and the member's own volume setting.
